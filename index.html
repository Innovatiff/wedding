A few things to know

Is bringing a plus one allowed at this event?

Your invitation will note whether a guest is included. If you are unsure, just ask us. We would love to help.

What would everyone plan to arrive?

Doors open at 3:30 pm and the ceremony begins promptly at 4:00 pm. Please arrive with a little time to spare.

Is there space for children?

We adore your little ones, but this will be an adults-only celebration. We hope you enjoy a night off.

Is there parking at the venue?

Yes, there is complimentary parking on site, and a shuttle from the recommended hotels.

Will there be vegetarian dishes served?

Absolutely. Let us know any dietary requirements in your RSVP and we will take care of the rest.
